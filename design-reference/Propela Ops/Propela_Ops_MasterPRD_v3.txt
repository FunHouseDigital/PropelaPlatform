1. Executive Summary
Propela Ops is Aya Yokwana's single operating environment as COO of Propela Health. It replaces
Notion for nurse tracking, extends into territory no current tool covers (institutional outreach
memory, nurse acquisition tracking, cohort delivery management), and is designed to eventually
replace Lightfield CRM in Phase 3.
The platform is built around four principles confirmed by real Propela data:
First, a nurse's record is not just a pipeline stage -- it is a combination of pipeline stage, readiness
status, next action, scorecard, and internal flags, all of which must be visible without scrolling.
Second, nurse acquisition happens across four distinct channels (organisations, referrals,
communities, events), each with a different relationship logic. Tracking them in one unified hub with
filters is more powerful than separate trackers.
Third, hospital partnerships are approached as information-sharing arrangements, not recruitment
outreach. Propela does not recruit nurses from hospitals directly -- it hosts information sessions for
nurses who are already interested in going international.
Fourth, nothing is deleted. Every nurse record, every outreach log, every flag stays in the system
permanently, with clear visual exit states.
2. Problem Statement
2.1 What is broken today
Nurse tracking has no single home. Notion holds the Cohort 1 pipeline. Spreadsheets hold scores.
Lightfield holds follow-ups. Gmail holds the communication history. Getting a complete picture of one
nurse requires switching between four tools.
The most critical operational field is buried. What does this nurse need from me right now is a text
field in a Notion row. It should be the first thing Aya sees on every nurse card, in large type, in colour.
Nurse acquisition has no memory. There is no record of which nursing schools, hospitals, WhatsApp
groups, or career fairs were engaged, what happened, what worked, and what to try again next
cohort. This knowledge resets every cycle.
Cohort delivery has no dashboard. Tracking 10 nurses through training, OET prep, results, and
placement happens manually. There is no view of cohort health, budget consumed, or upcoming
milestones.
Dropped and rejected nurses disappear. When a nurse is rejected or drops out, their record becomes
inaccessible. This loses relationship history, potential for future cohorts, and data on why the funnel
shaped the way it did.

8. Module 3 -- Nurse Acquisition Hub
8.1 Purpose
Track every channel through which Propela finds nurses. Four tracks, one platform, universal filter
and shared outreach log. Builds acquisition memory that compounds across cohort cycles.
Capacity: 300 records across all tracks
Important framing for hospital outreach: Propela does not contact hospital HR departments to recruit
nurses. The approach is a Partnership Track -- Propela partners with a nursing manager or matron to
host a voluntary information session for nurses already curious about international careers. The
nurses opt in. The outreach message to the hospital contact is: "We'd love to host a 30-minute
information session for any of your nurses interested in working abroad."

Active Ongoing relationship generating nurse leads
Dormant Previously engaged, no current activity
Closed Not viable -- do not re-engage

13. Design Principles
Brand:
• Primary: Propela Purple #5B2D8E
• Font: Poppins throughout
• Module accent colours: Nurse Database = teal, Organisations = orange, Community = emerald,
Referral = coral, Events = indigo, Cohort = deep purple
• Visual quality must be premium -- startup-grade, not a scratchpad
Next Action is the most prominent element on every nurse card. Colour, size, and position reflect
urgency. Red = overdue. Amber = due today. Teal = upcoming. Grey = no action.
Readiness Status is the second most prominent badge. Not Ready (grey), Placement Ready (green),
Placed (purple).
Flags are never hidden. Red card background for Dropped Out. Flag count in card header wherever
flags exist. Dashboard Red Flags feed catches stragglers.
Nothing is deleted. Exit states are permanent records. The filter can exclude them from view, but the
default includes them.
One action = one click or drag. Pipeline stage change: drag the card. Next Action update: single
dropdown select. Outreach log: one form, under 30 seconds.
Photo-first gallery. The gallery view is the default nurse view. It is designed to mirror the Airtable
NurseSync visual Aya built -- photo, stars, badges, location -- adapted for international placement
context.
Mobile-aware for key actions. Dashboard, nurse card header, and outreach log must function on
mobile. Full table and gallery views are desktop-first.
